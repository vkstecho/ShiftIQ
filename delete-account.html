🗑️ Account Deletion

How to delete your ShiftIQ account and data

This page explains how to permanently delete your ShiftIQ account, what data gets removed, what may be retained, and how long the process takes. This information is provided in compliance with Google Play Store data safety requirements and India's DPDP Act 2023.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion through any of these three methods:

1In-App (Recommended)
  1. Open the ShiftIQ app and log in with your registered mobile number
  2. Tap your name in the top-right corner to open Profile
  3. Scroll down and tap "🗑️ Delete My Account"
  4. Read the warning carefully
  5. Type the word DELETE (in capitals) in the confirmation box
  6. Tap "Delete My Account Permanently"

⏱ Processing time: Immediate. Your account is deleted within seconds.

2Email Request

Send an email to vivekkumarshukla@vkstech.com from your registered email or include your registered mobile number, with the subject line:

Subject: Delete My ShiftIQ Account

⏱ Processing time: Up to 72 hours.

3WhatsApp Request

Send a WhatsApp message from your registered mobile number to:

+91 89293 94920

⏱ Processing time: Up to 72 hours.

What Data Gets Deleted

If You Are a Team Member

When you delete your account:

  • ✅ Your profile (name, mobile number, employee code) is permanently removed
  • ✅ Your login credentials are revoked immediately
  • ✅ Your personal usage logs are removed
  • ⚠️ Historical schedule entries created by your manager remain in the company's records (anonymized — your name shown as "Deleted User")

If You Are a Manager

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Deleting a Manager account will permanently delete the entire company workspace, including all team members' data, schedules, leaves, and reports. This cannot be undone.
  • ✅ Your profile is permanently removed
  • ✅ All employees you registered are removed
  • ✅ All schedule data, leave applications, NCRs, reports, to-do items are deleted
  • ✅ Team members lose access to the app immediately
  • ✅ Company workspace is completely purged

If you want to transfer ownership instead of deleting, please contact us before requesting deletion.

Recovery Window

⚠️ No recovery available. Once deletion is processed, your data cannot be recovered. There is no grace period or undo option.

If you change your mind after requesting deletion via email/WhatsApp (and within 72 hours of submission), contact us immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete just some of my data instead of the whole account?

Yes — for partial data deletion (e.g. delete a specific shift entry but keep your profile), contact us via email and specify what you want removed.

Will my mobile number be available for re-registration after deletion?

Yes. After complete deletion, you can register again with the same mobile number as a new user.

What happens to WhatsApp messages I've already received?

WhatsApp messages are stored on your device and on WhatsApp's servers, not ours. Deleting your ShiftIQ account does not delete WhatsApp message history. You can delete WhatsApp messages directly through WhatsApp.

Can someone else request deletion of my account?

No. Only you (verified via OTP to your registered mobile number) or our Grievance Officer (in case of court order) can request deletion.

VKS Tech ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ShiftIQ application ("App"), available on the web and Google Play Store.

By using ShiftIQ, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Mobile Number — for login and OTP verification (mandatory)
  • Full Name — for identification within your team
  • Company Name & Type — to create your workspace (Manager registration only)
  • Employee Code — your internal identifier within the company
  • Designation / Job Title — for organizational hierarchy display
  • Schedule & Attendance Data — shifts, leaves, off-days you create or that your manager creates for you

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Info — browser type, OS version, screen size (for compatibility)
  • Usage Logs — login time, last active time
  • Crash Reports — anonymous error data to fix bugs (no personal content)

1.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or microphone
  • ❌ We do not collect financial/banking information
  • ❌ We do not access your device storage beyond what you explicitly upload
  • ❌ We do not track you across other apps or websites

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you via OTP sent to your mobile number
  • To provide shift schedule, leave, and reporting features
  • To send WhatsApp notifications when your shift changes (only to your registered number)
  • To enable your manager/admin to coordinate the team
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations (when required by Indian law)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase Realtime Database (Asia–Southeast region, Singapore data center) using industry-standard encryption.

  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication uses Firebase Phone Auth with reCAPTCHA protection
  • Database access is restricted by security rules — each company can only access its own data
  • Multi-tenant isolation prevents one company from accessing another's data
Note: While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but follow best practices to minimize risk.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Within your company — Manager can see team members' schedules; team members see their own schedules
  • Service Providers — Google Firebase (database & auth), Fast2SMS / Firebase SMS (OTP delivery), WhatsApp (you click links to share shift updates)
  • Legal Compliance — if required by court order or law enforcement under Indian law
  • Business Transfer — if VKS Tech is acquired or merged, your data may transfer with the same protections

5. Your Rights

Under Indian law (DPDP Act 2023) and standard privacy norms, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — fix inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete — permanently remove your account (see Section 7)
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the app at any time
  • Grievance redressal — contact our Grievance Officer (see Section 9)

6. Children's Privacy

ShiftIQ is intended for users 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If a parent/guardian discovers their child has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account immediately.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In-app: Profile → Delete My Account → Type "DELETE" to confirm
  • Via email: Send request to vivekkumarshukla@vkstech.com from your registered mobile or email
  • Via WhatsApp: Message +91 89293 94920

Upon deletion request, we will:

  • Permanently remove your profile, login data, and personal information within 72 hours

  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days

For more details, see our Account Deletion page.

8. Data Retention

  • While your account is active, we retain your data to provide the service
  • If your account is inactive for 365 days, we may auto-delete it after notification
  • If you request deletion, see Section 7

  • Remember your language preference (Hindi/English)
  • Cache app shell for offline use (Service Worker)

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising trackers.
